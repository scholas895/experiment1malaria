# Data

Place BdiDATASETS.xlsx in data/raw/ before running the pipeline.

The workbook is NOT distributed here because it contains district-level
malaria surveillance data subject to institutional data-sharing policies.

## Expected sheets

- 2022, 2023, 2024 - monthly cases per health district
- precipitationmensuel30years - rainfall per station
- Temperaturemoyen - monthly min/max/mean temperature
- Humiditerelatives  stations - monthly relative humidity
- Niveau du Lac Tanganyika - monthly lake level
- Niveau d'eau de la RiviereRusiz - river level & discharge
